Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Wilmington, Wilmington Restoration Co answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Wilmington

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Know the location and condition of your sewer cleanout for emergency access
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
