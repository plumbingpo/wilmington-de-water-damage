Risk Assessment

Risk Assessments That Help Prevent Commercial Water Damage

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Sudden failures caused by old plumbing, poor waterproofing, or moisture nobody can see may lead to property damage, injuries, and expensive lawsuits. A Wilmington Restoration Co risk assessment looks at every part of the property for exposure to water intrusion, with moisture monitoring tools and thermal imaging doing the detection. You receive a written report that lists each risk, ranks it by priority, and spells out the preventive steps to take.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Owners who keep up regular professional inspections and follow the experts' advice are viewed favorably by insurers and courts. Our recommendation is a moisture assessment once a year for every commercial property, and more frequent checks in high-risk spots like mechanical rooms, basements, and older plumbing systems.
