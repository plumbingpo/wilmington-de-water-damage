The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

This is not a job to take on yourself. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Give the crawl space proper ventilation, and think about adding a vapor barrier over the soil
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
  • Deal quickly with condensation that forms on pipes, cold surfaces or window glass
  • In bathrooms, basements and other damp areas, choose paint and drywall rated mold-resistant
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
